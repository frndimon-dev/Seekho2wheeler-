01Our story

Everyone deserves the freedom to ride

What began as a small training ground in Paschim Putiary, Tollygunge, is now seven centres across Kolkata, Howrah and North 24 Parganas.

Around 70% of our learners are women, and many start from zero. That is why every class is calm, step by step and one-to-one with a trainer.

02What we stand for

How we teach

Beginners firstTrainers who specialise in complete beginners, even if you never rode a cycle.
RespectfulLadies batches and a calm, supportive setting from the first class.
Step by stepBalance and control first on a quiet lane, then real Kolkata roads.

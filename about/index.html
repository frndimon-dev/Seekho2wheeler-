About Seekho 2 Wheeler

Empowering independence through safe riding — scooty and bike training across Kolkata since 2018.

Our story

Seekho started in 2018 with one simple belief: everyone deserves the freedom to ride. What began as a small training ground in Paschim Putiary, Tollygunge, is now seven centres across Kolkata, Howrah and North 24 Parganas.

Our mission

To give every learner — especially women and people who never rode a cycle — the skills and confidence to ride on Kolkata roads on their own, safely.

What makes us different

  • Patient trainers who specialise in complete beginners
  • Ladies batches in a respectful, supportive setting
  • Step by step: balance and control first, then real roads
  • Seven centres, open every day 7 am – 7 pm

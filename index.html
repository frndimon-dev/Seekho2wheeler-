FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers before you call us.

I can't ride a cycle. Can I still learn a scooty?

Yes. Many of our learners started without knowing how to ride a cycle. We begin with balance and slow control, then move to real roads step by step.

Can women learn in a safe, comfortable environment?

Yes. We have ladies batches and patient trainers who focus on confidence and safety from the very first class.

How long does it take, and what are the timings?

It depends on your progress; most courses are a set number of one-hour classes. Morning and evening slots are available every day; call us for the exact timing at your centre.

Do you provide the scooty or bike?

Yes. You practise on our vehicles, so you do not need your own to start. Bike training runs at Tollygunge and Barasat.

Is real road and traffic practice included?

Yes. Once you have basic control, your trainer takes you on guided real-traffic practice so you become road-ready.

What are the fees, and how do I book?

Courses start from ₹2,500 (RTO preparation from ₹2,800; doorstep from ₹4,500, by distance). Call 97484 81630, WhatsApp us or use the booking page — we confirm your centre and slot.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

This policy explains how Kolkata Scooty Bike Training Centre, trading as Seekho 2 Wheeler ("we", "us"), handles information on seekho2wheeler.com and in the tools we use to run our business. Our main centre is at 1, 78, Banerjee Para Rd, Haridevpur, Paschim Putiary, Kolkata 700041.

What we collect

  • When you book or ask about a class (booking form, WhatsApp or phone call): your name, phone number, area and the course you are interested in. The booking form opens WhatsApp with your message; nothing is stored on this website.
  • During training: details you share with us to arrange your classes, such as preferred timing and centre.
  • Photos and videos of classes are taken and shared on our pages only with the learner's permission.

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

How we use it

Only to reply to you, arrange and run your classes, and keep you informed about your training. We do not sell, rent or trade your information, and we do not share it with anyone except where the law requires it.

Google Business Profile tool

We use a private internal tool ("Business Desk") that connects to Google APIs with our own Google account, only to manage our own Google Business Profile: publishing updates and replying to reviews. Data received from Google APIs (such as our profile details and the public reviews left on our profile) is used only for this purpose, is not shared with or sold to third parties, and is not used for advertising. Our use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Access can be removed at any time from the Google account's security settings.

How long we keep it

We keep booking details only as long as needed for your training and our normal business records, then delete them.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you. Call or WhatsApp us on +91 97484 81630.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Why Women Should Learn Two Wheeler Riding

Learning to ride is more than a skill — it is independence. Office, market, the children's school: you go on your own time, without waiting for anyone.

1. Independence on your own schedule

No more waiting for a lift or an auto. A scooty makes daily travel in Kolkata quicker and cheaper.

2. Ladies batches

Around 70% of our learners are women. Our ladies batches and patient trainers make the first classes comfortable.

3. Start from absolute zero

Never rode a cycle? Many of our women learners started exactly there.

4. Guided practice and flexible slots

Morning and evening slots fit around work and home, and your trainer stays beside you on real roads.
